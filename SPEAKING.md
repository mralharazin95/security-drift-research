## Topics

- **AI Product Engineering:** moving from prototype to governed, testable, accountable delivery
- **Security Drift:** how vulnerabilities can accumulate across iterative AI-assisted development
- **Digital Transformation:** aligning operating models, technology decisions, and delivery governance
- **Project Leadership:** decision gates, risk ownership, quality controls, and cross-functional execution
- **Quality and Security by Design:** building assurance into product iterations rather than adding it at the end
- **Responsible AI for Commerce and FinTech:** data boundaries, evaluation, human escalation, and operational accountability

## Suggested Sessions

### From AI Feature to Governed Product: Five Gates Before Go-Live

A practical framework covering business outcomes, data and trust boundaries, quality evaluation, security and privacy, and operational accountability. Attendees leave with a go-live checklist for deciding when an AI feature is ready for customers and when it should remain a controlled pilot.

### Security Drift in AI-Assisted Development

An evidence-based explanation of how small omissions can accumulate across iterative development cycles, how the Security Drift Index (SDI) measures directional change in vulnerability profiles, and where security checkpoints belong in the delivery workflow.

> The associated Security Drift manuscript is under review at *Array* and is not published. The public research artifact and reproducibility materials are available in this repository.

### Digital Transformation Is an Operating-Model Decision

A management-focused session on why transformation efforts fail when tools change faster than ownership, controls, incentives, and decision rights.
